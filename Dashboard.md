## Exam structure

| Paper | Code | Theme | Duration | Marks | % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | 1BS0/01 | Investigating small business | 1h45 | 90 | 50% |
| Paper 2 | 1BS0/02 | Building a business | 1h45 | 90 | 50% |

Both papers: Section A (35) + Section B (30) + Section C (25). Marks per question: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 12.

> [!tip] Almost-Certain topics — drill these first
> These appear on **every** paper or in 80%+ of sittings. If you only have a week, work down this list:
> 1. **Marketing mix** (1.4.3 / 2.2) — every paper, often a 9-mark Justify
> 2. **Sources of finance** (1.3.5 / 2.4) — every paper, often the Q6c Justify pairing
> 3. **Bar-gate stock graphs** (2.3.2) — 5 of 6 live Paper 2s, virtually guaranteed
> 4. **Gross profit / Net profit / GP margin / NP margin** (2.4.1) — every Paper 2
> 5. **The two 9-mark Justify formats** — one in Section B, one in Section C, every paper
> 6. **The 12-mark Evaluate format** — Q7e, every paper, must end with "It depends on…"
