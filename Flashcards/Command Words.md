# Command Words (Spec Appendix 1, p.29)

One card per command word from the Edexcel 1BS0 spec. Question, then `?`, then answer, separated by `---`.

---

What does a Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) require, and how many marks?
?
Select one or more correct answers from a choice of options. Tests recall of knowledge from the spec. Worth 1 mark (single answer) or 2 marks (multi-answer / "select two").

---

What does the command word "Define" require, and how many marks?
?
Define a term from the specification content — give the precise meaning in one sentence. Worth 1 mark.

---

What does the command word "Give" require, and how many marks?
?
Give an answer testing recall of knowledge from the spec — short, factual, no context needed. Worth 1 mark.

---

What does the command word "State" require, and how many marks?
?
Give an answer no longer than a sentence, referring to a piece of contextual information from the case study. Tests AO2 — generic answers score 0. Worth 1 mark.

---

What does the command word "Identify" require, and how many marks?
?
Select the correct answer from reading a graph or table of data (e.g. identify the highest month, smallest segment). Worth 1 mark.

---

What does the command word "Calculate" require, and how many marks?
?
Use mathematical skills to reach an answer based on given data. Calculators may be used and workings should be shown — examiners reward method marks even if the final figure is wrong. Worth 2 (occasionally 3) marks.

---

What does the command word "Complete the table" require, and how many marks?
?
Work out the values missing from a presented table of data (e.g. fill in two missing cells of a cash-flow forecast). Worth 2 marks (one per cell).

---

What does the command word "Outline" require, and how many marks?
?
Give two linked points about a business concept or issue, placed in context (i.e. applied to the case study). Worth 2 marks.

---

What does the command word "Explain" require, and how many marks?
?
Give a statement of fact, with two further expansion points that may expand on each other or both from the same fact. No case-study context needed. Use linking words (because, leads to, therefore). Worth 3 marks.

---

What does the command word "Discuss" require, and how many marks?
?
Write an extended answer requiring expansion and exploration of a business concept or issue. No case-study context — students may bring an example for illustration. Worth 6 marks (always Q3e in Section A).

---

What does the command word "Analyse" require, and how many marks?
?
Write an extended answer requiring expansion and exploration of a business concept or issue, placed in context by the question (named case-study business). Worth 6 marks (Section B Q4b and Q5c).

---

What does the command word "Justify" require, and how many marks?
?
Write an extended answer using information provided to recommend ONE of two options to a business owner. Argue for the chosen option; use the rejected option to strengthen your choice. Worth 9 marks (Section B Q6c and Section C Q7d).

---

What does the command word "Evaluate" require, and how many marks?
?
Write an extended answer using knowledge of spec content to reach a supported conclusion about a business situation. Must end with "It depends on…" + new evaluative content (not a summary). Worth 12 marks — always the final question Q7e.

---

How are the 9 marks for a Justify question split?
?
3 marks Knowledge (AO1) + 3 marks Application to business context (AO2) + 3 marks Analysis/Judgement (AO3a/b). Skipping any tier caps the mark.

---

How are the 12 marks for an Evaluate question split, and what is the structure?
?
3 AO1 (Knowledge) + 3 AO2 (Application) + 3 AO3a (Analysis) + 3 AO3b (Evaluation/Conclusion). Structure: intro → 2 PEEL paragraphs (each balanced) → "It depends on…" conclusion bringing NEW evaluation (magnitude, timing, significance).
