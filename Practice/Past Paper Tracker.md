# Past Paper Tracker

Every past paper available in your archive, plus the embargoed June 2025 series. Tick the box when attempted, fill in your raw mark, calculate the percentage, and note any topic where you lost more than 2 marks so you can come back to it later.

> [!tip] How to use this tracker
> 1. Pick a paper. Print or open the QP. Set a 1h45 timer.
> 2. Sit it under exam conditions — no notes, no pause.
> 3. Mark using the **MS** (mark scheme) link. Be strict — exam markers are.
> 4. Read the **ER** (examiner report) for that paper — it tells you exactly where most students lost marks. If you lost marks in the same place, that's your weak area.
> 5. Add the topic to the **Weak Areas** column. Revisit it on the [[Practice/Revision Checklist]].
> 6. Aim to finish 4 full papers (= 2 full series) before the exam — don't burn through every paper at once; leave 2 series for the final fortnight.

> [!info] Mark conversion
> 90 marks per paper. 180 marks total per series. Grade-9 boundary historically ~143/180; grade-7 ~110/180; grade-5 ~78/180 (2024 boundaries — see `Archive/support/`).
