# Command Words

> [!info] Source
> Official taxonomy from **Appendix 1, page 29 of the Edexcel GCSE Business 1BS0 Specification (Issue 2, July 2022)**. The wording below matches Pearson's own definitions; the templates and "what examiners want" notes come from cross-paper examiner reports.

## The full command-word table

| Command | Mark size | What Pearson says it requires | What examiners want | 1-line answer template |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MCQ / Which one** | 1–2 | Select the correct answer from a choice | Recall — no working needed | "[A/B/C/D]" — circle the letter; for 2-mark "select two", tick exactly two |
| **Define** | 1 | Define a term from the spec | Pure recall of the spec definition | "[Term] is …" |
| **Give** | 1 | Give an answer testing recall | Knowledge only — no application | "[Item]" — single word/phrase |
| **State** | 1 | Give an answer (≤1 sentence) referring to **contextual** information from which students must select the answer | **AO2 application** — generic answer = 0 marks | "[Specific feature from the case study, named]" |
| **Identify** | 1 | Select the correct answer from a graph or table | Read the chart/table and pick out the value | "[Value/label] from the chart" |
| **Calculate** | 2–3 | Use mathematical skills to reach the answer; calculator allowed; **workings should be given** | Show every step — method marks awarded even if final figure wrong | Workings line by line, then "Answer = £X / X% / X units" |
| **Complete the table** | 2 | Work out missing values from a presented table | Fill cells; check totals reconcile | Write directly into the table cells |
| **Outline** | 2 | Give two **linked** points about a concept, **placed in context** | Point 1 + linking word + point 2 (context required) | "[Point 1], which means [point 2 applied to business]" |
| **Explain** | 3 | Statement of fact with **two further expansion points**; no context | Use BLT: **B**ecause … **L**eads **T**o … | "One reason is X. This is because Y, which leads to Z." |
| **Discuss** | 6 | Extended answer requiring expansion and exploration of a concept; **no context** required (illustration optional) | One PEEL paragraph or two short PEEs; balanced exploration | Intro → PEEL → PEEL → mini-conclusion |
| **Analyse** | 6 | Extended answer requiring expansion and exploration; **placed in context by the question** | Apply to the named business; chains of cause-and-effect | PEEL: Point → Evidence (from extract) → Explain (chain) → Link to business outcome |
| **Justify** | 9 | Extended answer using information provided to **recommend ONE of two options** | Pick one, defend it, dismiss the other in one line; **never balance both** | Intro stating choice → PEEL for chosen → 1-line dismissal of rejected → contextual conclusion |
| **Evaluate** | 12 | Extended answer using knowledge to reach a **supported conclusion** about a business situation | Two PEEL paragraphs (one each side) + an **"It depends on…"** conclusion bringing **new evaluative content** | Intro → PEEL for → PEEL against → "It depends on [factor 1] and [factor 2], so on balance…" |

## Quick rules from examiner reports

> [!warning] Top mark-saving habits
> 1. **State** = AO2. If the case-study business is named in italics, your answer must reference that business's specific context. Generic answers score **0**.
> 2. **Explain** = three marks of three separate ideas. Use **BLT** chains ("Because… Leads To…") to force expansion.
> 3. **Calculate** = always show workings; **method marks** are given for correct method even if the final number is wrong. Always write the unit (£, %, units).
> 4. **Justify** = one-sided. Pick the option in your **first sentence** and never look back. The rejected option's drawbacks support your chosen option — they are NOT a separate paragraph.
> 5. **Evaluate** = the conclusion **must bring NEW evaluative content** ("it depends on size, time horizon, level of competition…"). A summary-only conclusion caps you at Level 2.

## Mark-time-budget rule
**1 mark = 1 minute.** A 12-mark Evaluate gets ~12 minutes; a 9-mark Justify ~9 minutes; a 6-mark Discuss/Analyse ~6 minutes; a 3-mark Explain ~2 minutes (don't overrun on short answers — they have a low ceiling).
