# Edexcel GCSE Business 1BS0 — Paper 2 ("Building a business") Cross-Year Analysis

**Scope:** Specimen (SAMs) + every available live series — June 2019, Nov 2020, Nov 2021, June 2022, June 2023, June 2024.
**Paper format:** 1h 45 (1h 30 in 2019–22; lengthened to 1h 45 from the SAMs Issue 2 / 2023 onwards) · 90 marks · three sections (A, B, C). Section B & C are case-study based; Section C usually involves a larger / multinational firm.

---

## 1. Question structure consistency

Every single sitting (2019–2024) follows the **same skeleton**:

| Section | Questions | Marks | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Q1, Q2, Q3 — three short-answer "stems" each with parts (a)–(e) | **35** | No case study. Mix of MCQs, calculations, "Explain one…" 3-mark items, and one 6-mark "Discuss" |
| **B** | Q4, Q5, Q6 — pinned to a short Extract A + figure(s) about a mid-sized / well-known UK/US plc | **30** | Builds: 2-mark "Outline" → 6-mark "Analyse" → calculation(s) → 9-mark "Justify" two options |
| **C** | Q7 only — pinned to Extract B + figure(s) about a larger / multinational | **25** | 1-mark define/identify, 1-mark chart read, 2-mark "Outline", 9-mark "Justify" *and* 12-mark "Evaluate" |

**Sub-question fingerprint (identical every year):**
- Q1 = 8 marks (1+1+3+3) · Q2 = 12 marks (2+2+2+3+3) · Q3 = 15 marks (1+2+3+3+6)
- Q4 = 8 marks (2+6) · Q5 = 10 marks (2+2+6 or 1+1+2+6) · Q6 = 12 marks (1+2+9)
- Q7 = 25 marks (1+1+2+9+12)

Section A always totals 35, B totals 30, C totals 25. The 6-mark "Discuss" in Q3(e), the 9-mark "Justify" in Q6(c) and Q7(d), and the 12-mark "Evaluate" in Q7(e) are **non-negotiable fixtures**.

**Specimen anomaly:** the SAMs paper merges the two 9-mark Justify questions into one (Q6(d) & Q7(d) — both 9 marks) and is the only paper where Q6 totals 12 marks via a 1+2+9 structure exactly as the live papers later adopted. Live papers have stuck rigidly to the SAMs skeleton.

**Observable patterns:**
- **HR and Operations dominate** every year (rarely <14 marks, often 20+).
- **Finance always appears**, anchored by at least one calculation in Section A and another in Section B; financial-source choice (Justify Option 1 vs 2) is a frequent 9-marker.
- **Marketing is steady** (always 14+) but the *specific* sub-topic rotates (design mix one year, pricing strategy the next, life cycle the next).
- **Growing the business** consistently powers Section C (takeover, plc, multinational, growth strategies).

## 3. Guaranteed question types (appear EVERY year)

1. **MCQs in Section A** — at least 2 single-answer (1 mark) and 2 multi-answer (2 mark) MCQs in Q1/Q2/Q3.
2. **A bar-gate stock graph** — appeared in SAMs (Fender), 2020 (KFC), 2022 (Q1 stem in Section A), 2023 (Section A Q3), 2024 (Section A Q3). Only 2019 and 2021 omitted it.
3. **Profit/margin calculation** — GP, NP, GP margin or NP margin appears in either Section A Q2(c) or Section B Q5; in 2024 the spec turned this into "average price" and "% of total order" instead, but profit-style calc has never been absent across all years and sections combined.
4. **A 6-mark "Discuss" in Section A Q3(e)** — locked-in.
5. **A 6-mark "Analyse" in Section B Q4(b) or Q5(c)** — locked-in.
6. **A 9-mark "Justify which one of these two options"** in Section B Q6(c) — every paper without exception.
7. **A 12-mark "Evaluate"** as the final question (Q7(e)) — locked-in. Always rubric "You should use the information provided as well as your knowledge of business."
8. **A "define/give one" 1-mark item** at the start of Q7 (define takeover / pressure group / multinational / give one stage of sales process / give one element of marketing mix / give one non-financial method to motivate).
9. **A chart reading 1-mark item** at Q7(b) — pie chart, bar chart, or line graph "identify the X with the largest/smallest…".
10. **A second 9-mark "Justify"** in Section C Q7(d) — present in every live paper (in SAMs it's the *only* Section C 9-marker; live papers add it back atop the 12-marker).

**Insights:**
- "**Explain one…**" is the single most common command word — five times per paper, every paper, always 3 marks.
- "**Justify**" appears exactly twice per paper (one in Section B, one in Section C).
- "**Discuss**" appears exactly once per paper (always Q3e, 6 marks, no case study — pure A01/A03a).
- "**Evaluate**" only ever appears as Q7(e), 12 marks.
- "**Outline**" (2 marks) is reserved for the easier marks at start of Q4 and Q6(b) / Q7(c).

- Net profit (2021, 2023)

- NP margin (2022, 2023)

**Examiner-noted weaknesses to drill** (taken from 2020 Paper 2 Examiner Report; the same patterns recur in later years):
- Students lose easy marks failing to **interpret bar-gate stock graphs** correctly (especially subtracting buffer stock from re-order level for "size of order").
- "**State…**" questions test AO2 (application) — must reference the specific business context (e.g. "Restaurant manager" not "manager").
- "**Justify**" questions only need to argue *for* the chosen option; one-sided generic responses score Level 1. Students should use the discarded option to *strengthen* the chosen one, not analyse both.
- "**Explain**" answers must use linking words (because, therefore, as a result) — examiners count chains of reasoning explicitly.
- The 12-mark Q7(e) requires a **conclusion that brings new evaluation** ("it depends on…"), not just a summary.
