# Cross-Paper Analysis — Edexcel GCSE Business 1BS0

Synthesis of `paper1_analysis.md` + `paper2_analysis.md` across all available years (Specimen Set 1 + Set 2, June 2019, Nov 2020, Nov 2021, June 2022, June 2023, June 2024) plus the 2021 new-format Paper 1 practice.

---

## 1. Combined paper structure (extremely stable)

| Feature | Paper 1 (1BS0/01) | Paper 2 (1BS0/02) |
|---|---|---|
| Theme | T1 Investigating small business | T2 Building a business |
| Duration | 1h45 | 1h45 |
| Marks | 90 | 90 |
| Sections | A 35 / B 30 / C 25 | A 35 / B 30 / C 25 |
| MCQs | 4 (1 mark each) | typically embedded as 1-mark items |
| 3-mark "Explain one…" | 6 in Section A | 5 in Section A |
| 6-mark "Analyse / Discuss" | 3 across paper | 2 across paper |
| 9-mark "Justify" | 2 (one in B, one in C) | 2 (Q6c and Q7d) |
| 12-mark "Evaluate" | 1 (final question) | 1 (Q7e, final question) |
| Section B context | Small UK business (sole trader / Ltd) | Mid-cap manufacturer or retailer |
| Section C context | Niche/tech/values-led start-up | plc / multinational |

**Constants worth memorising:**
- 1 mark ≈ 1 minute is the timing rule; the 12-mark and two 9-mark questions need ~10 + 9 + 9 = 28 minutes between them on each paper
- Paper 2 has a **bar-gate stock graph in 5 of 6 live papers** — virtually guaranteed
- Paper 1 always has **4 MCQs + 4 calculations** drawn from a stable bank
- Section C of Paper 2 is **always** a plc dilemma (Sainsbury's → Iceland → ASOS → JD Sports → Facebook → Cineworld)

## 7. Command-word distribution (combined)

| Command | Approx % of paper marks |
|---|---|
| Justify (9-mark) | ~20% — TWO 9-markers per paper |
| Evaluate (12-mark) | ~13% — one 12-mark per paper |
| Discuss / Analyse (6-mark) | ~13–16% — 2-3 per paper |
| Explain (3-mark) | ~17–20% — 5-6 per paper |
| Calculate (2-3 mark) | ~10% — guaranteed by spec |
| Outline (2-mark) | ~5–10% |
| State / Identify / Define / MCQ (1 mark) | ~10–15% |

**Strategic implication:** the 9-mark and 12-mark extended responses together are ~33% of the paper. Drilling these single-handedly moves grades the most.

## 9. Examiner-flagged student weaknesses (recurring themes 2019-2024)

1. **Justify ≠ balanced essay** — Justify is one-sided: pick the option, defend it, dismiss the other in one line
2. **12-mark Evaluate must end "It depends on…"** with a contextual final judgement, not a generic summary
3. **Bar-gate stock graphs** — students forget to subtract buffer stock when reading reorder level
4. **Application marks lost** — generic "the manager should" instead of "Greggs' regional bakery manager should"
5. **Mark scheme weighting** — 9-mark = 3 marks knowledge + 3 marks application + 3 marks analysis/eval. Skipping any one tier caps the mark
6. **Calculation working** — examiners reward method marks even if the final answer is wrong. Show every step

## 10. Bottom-line revision priorities for Nicole

**Top 5 things to drill before the exam:**
1. The **two 9-mark Justify** structures (intro → option chosen with PEEL → reject other → conclusion)
2. The **12-mark Evaluate** structure (intro → 2 PEEL paragraphs → "it depends on…" conclusion)
3. **Calculation bank**: GP/NP/margins, ARR, average, % change, break-even, cash flow
4. **Bar-gate stock graphs** for Paper 2
5. **Marketing mix** with technology twist (digital marketing, e-commerce) — comes up everywhere

**Top 3 case-study habits:**
1. Always name the actual business in every sentence of an extended response
2. Always end the 12-mark with "It depends on…" + 1-2 contextual factors
3. Use the source booklet / case-study text to lift specific data points (numbers, %s, named products)
