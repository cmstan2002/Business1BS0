Subject: GCSE Edexcel Business (2017 spec, code 1BS0)
- Paper 1 = 1BS0/01 "Investigating small business" (May/June)
- Paper 2 = 1BS0/02 "Building a business" (June)
- First exam: June 2019

URL host: `https://qualifications.pearson.com`
PDF base path: `/content/dam/pdf/GCSE/Business/2017/`
File naming pattern (exam materials):
- Question paper: `1BS0_<paper>_que_YYYYMMDD.pdf` or `1bs0-<paper>-que-YYYYMMDD.pdf`
- Mark scheme: `1BS0_<paper>_rms_YYYYMMDD.pdf` (June series) / `1BS0_<paper>_msc_YYYYMMDD.pdf` (some autumn re-sits)
- Examiners' report (Principal Examiner Feedback): `1BS0_<paper>_pef_YYYYMMDD.pdf`
- Note: file names use underscores up to the November 2021 series and hyphens from June 2022 onwards. Path case ("exam-materials" vs "Exam-materials") varies by year.

## Notes & Gaps

- **2020 (Summer)**: No live exams (COVID cancellations). Pearson released the Nov 2020 series instead.
- **2021 (Summer)**: No traditional live exams (COVID - TAGs used). Pearson published "new format" practice papers (`pearson-edexcel-gcse-business-2021-paper1-questionpaper-newformat.pdf`); a paper-2 equivalent is suspected but not confirmed at the analogous URL.
- **2025 (Summer)**: June 2025 grade-boundaries PDF exists, but the 1BS0 question papers / mark schemes for June 2025 were not exposed via Google (typically released Aug/Sep; embargo for ~9 months). Try `1bs0-01-que-20250515.pdf` style URL after Sep 2025.
- **2022 Nov mark schemes (1BS0_02_rms_20220224.pdf)**: Listed in pattern but WebFetch returned 404. The corresponding paper 1 file for that date was also 404 in fetch but Google indexed the paper 1 examiner report. Likely Pearson stored the mark schemes under a slightly different filename (could be `msc` rather than `rms`). Worth re-trying with `1BS0_01_msc_20220224.pdf` and `1BS0_02_msc_20220224.pdf`.
- **Inserts / Source booklets for live papers**: From 2023 the case-study material is inside the question paper; from 2025 it ships as a separate insert booklet. The SAM source booklets above are the only standalone inserts found at the spec-and-sample path.
- **Pre-release material**: For GCSE Business 1BS0 there is no pre-release material in the GCE sense; the practice "new-format" papers from 2021 are the closest analogue.
- **Path case sensitivity**: Pearson serves the same files at both `/exam-materials/` (lowercase) and `/Exam-materials/` (capital E) on different years. Both case forms verified to work for files originally listed under each.
